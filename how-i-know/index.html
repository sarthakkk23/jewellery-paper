How I know, in plain words.

Every number on this site comes from a published record and is checked by code. Tap a method to see how it works.

Share

Out of every hundred, how many. Most figures here are shares: of exports, of imports, of licences.

Change on a year earlier

The same months this year against last year, so the season doesn't fool you.

Averages over a few months

One month can swing; five months together are steadier. That's why periods are named beside each share.

Rounding

Figures are rounded for reading, so parts may not add up to exactly 100.

Checked twice

Every figure is computed by code, then recomputed from the raw tables by separate code before it goes up.

Each number carries a label. Measured: read straight from a published source, named under it. Arithmetic: a sum anyone can redo from the figures shown. Estimate: rests on an assumption, which is stated. Judgement: my view, reasoned from the above. Example: invented, only to show a method.

Aggregates only. I describe the trade, never a named business, and never criticise a business, area or community.

Found a mistake? Tell me, and I'll correct it and note the change.
