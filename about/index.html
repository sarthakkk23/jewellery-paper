Why I'm doing this.

I'm Sarthak Oberoi, a data analyst in Jaipur. Jaipur's jewellery trade is old, large and talked about, but rarely measured. I wanted to see what the public records say about it, put the numbers in one place, and keep them current. This is free. Nothing is for sale.

The house rules.

  • Aggregates only.Figures describe the trade, never one business.
  • Praise, never criticism.A business is named only to credit it, with a public source.
  • Nobody pays.No business or body pays for this work or to appear in it.
  • Every number checked.Each figure is recomputed by separate code before it goes up.
