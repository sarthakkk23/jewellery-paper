What the records say.

What Jaipur's jewellery trade looks like in export tables, US import data, auction results, licence counts and gold rates. Each finding is one sentence and one number, with its chart where a picture says it faster, and how I know.

59.5%

59.5% of what Jaipur's export zone ships is gold jewellery. Coloured gemstones are 10%.

In FY2023-24 plain gold was 46.2% of the Jaipur SEZ's gem and jewellery exports, studded gold 13.3% and silver 24.8%. Worked coloured gemstones were 10%.

The opening figure

How I know
  • GJEPC's SEZ booklet, Table 7. This is the SEZ only, not all of Jaipur: most of the city's stone trade ships from outside it.

  • 10% Measured: Worked coloured gemstones as a share of the Jaipur SEZ's exports. FY2023-24, Jaipur SEZ. GJEPC, Gem & jewellery exports from SEZs (booklet, March 2025), Table 7, p. 34.
  • 46.2% Measured: Plain gold jewellery as a share of the Jaipur SEZ's exports. FY2023-24, Jaipur SEZ. GJEPC, Gem & jewellery exports from SEZs (booklet, March 2025), Table 7, p. 34.
  • 13.3% Arithmetic: Studded gold jewellery as a share of the Jaipur SEZ's exports. FY2023-24, Jaipur SEZ. GJEPC, Gem & jewellery exports from SEZs (booklet, March 2025), Table 7, p. 34.

59.5%

Gold is 59.5% of the export zone's shipments. A decade ago, coloured gemstones were about 46% of the city's exports.

The two figures cover different things (the whole city in 2014-15, the SEZ in FY2023-24), so read them as a contrast, not a trend line.

The export mix

0.3%

The Rajasthan region's exports jumped 82%. Gemstones explain about 0.3% of it.

Between April and September 2025, the region's gem and jewellery exports rose by US$550 million on a year earlier. India's coloured-gemstone exports rose so little in the same months that they can't plausibly account for it. The region now ships 8.7% of India's gem and jewellery exports.

The boom isn't stones

How I know
  • GJEPC's region tables. "About", not "at most": the gemstone figure is India's net change, not the region's. GJEPC reports this as the Rajasthan region, so I do too.
  • 82% Arithmetic: Rise in the Rajasthan region's gem and jewellery exports. April-September 2025 against a year earlier, Rajasthan region (GJEPC). GJEPC quarterly and monthly trade reports, region tables.
  • about 0.3% Arithmetic: India's rise in coloured-gemstone exports as a share of the Rajasthan region's rise. April-September 2025 against a year earlier, Rajasthan region (GJEPC). GJEPC quarterly and monthly trade reports, region tables. About, not at most: the gemstone figure is India's net change, so it isn't a ceiling.
  • US$550 million Arithmetic: Rise in the Rajasthan region's exports, in dollars. April-September 2025 against a year earlier, Rajasthan region (GJEPC). GJEPC quarterly and monthly trade reports, region tables.
  • 8.7% Arithmetic: The Rajasthan region's share of India's gem and jewellery exports. April-September 2025, Rajasthan region (GJEPC). GJEPC quarterly and monthly trade reports, region tables.

60%

During the 50% tariff, 60% of America's silver jewellery came from countries other than India, Thailand and China. A year earlier it was 29%.

Thailand didn't take India's place: its share fell too, from 34% to 29%. Periods compared: September 2025 to January 2026, against September 2024 to January 2025.

The US kept buying silver, from someone else

How I know

4.4%

India's share of America's gold jewellery fell from 26.3% to 4.4%. In March 2026 it turned.

It went from 3.5% in February 2026 to 11.2% in March, the first full month India paid the same 10% as nearly everyone else.

Gold fell away, then turned

How I know
  • UN Comtrade, US imports by country. The US code for gold jewellery (HS 711319) also includes platinum jewellery.

Every commercial-quality emerald auction from the Zambian mine since 2022 was viewed only in Jaipur: 9 of 9.

Buyers come to Jaipur to see the rough, then bid online. Higher-quality rough is viewed in Bangkok.

How I know

US$9.72

Commercial-quality emerald rough reached US$9.72 a carat, up 117% in two years.

The August-September 2026 auction set the highest commercial-quality average in Gemfields' auction record, which began in 2009.

6 times

November brings about 6 times as many foreign visits to Rajasthan as June.

Over 2023-2025, November carried 14.3% of the state's foreign tourist visits and June 2.4%. In 2025, January-March and November-December together carried 64%.

The tourist calendar

How I know
  • Rajasthan Tourism's annual progress reports, monthly tables. These are visits, not tourists (one person seeing two cities counts twice), and state figures, not Jaipur's.
  • about 6 times Arithmetic: November's foreign visits as a multiple of June's. 2023-2025, Rajasthan. Rajasthan Tourism Department, annual progress reports 2018-19 to 2025-26 (monthly visits tables).
  • 14.3% Arithmetic: November's share of Rajasthan's foreign tourist visits. 2023-2025, Rajasthan. Rajasthan Tourism Department, annual progress reports 2018-19 to 2025-26 (monthly visits tables). Visits, not tourists: one person seeing two cities counts twice. State figures, not Jaipur's.
  • 2.4% Arithmetic: June's share of Rajasthan's foreign tourist visits. 2023-2025, Rajasthan. Rajasthan Tourism Department, annual progress reports 2018-19 to 2025-26 (monthly visits tables).
  • 64% Arithmetic: Share of Rajasthan's 2025 foreign visits in January-March and November-December. 2025, Rajasthan. Rajasthan Tourism Department, annual progress reports 2018-19 to 2025-26 (monthly visits tables).

For everyone. The close.

27

I found 27 published figures for how many people or businesses Jaipur's jewellery trade has. None of them is a count.

The rest are estimates, repeated from one report to the next. The familiar "two lakh workers" appears in three studies, and none of them counted anyone.

The trade nobody has counted

How I know
  • My review of every study, report and article I could find with a figure for Jaipur's trade. The 2013 Economic Census did count establishments, but for the state, not Jaipur.
  • 27 Arithmetic: Published figures I found for how many people work in Jaipur's jewellery trade, or how many units it has. all years to October 2026, Jaipur. My review of every published study, report and article giving a figure for Jaipur's jewellery trade. "For Jaipur" and "that I found": the 2013 Economic Census is a direct count, but at state level.
  • None Arithmetic: Of those, figures that are a direct count by the source. all years to October 2026, Jaipur. My review of every published study, report and article giving a figure for Jaipur's jewellery trade.

So I'm counting it. The first census of Jaipur's jewellery businesses comes next.
