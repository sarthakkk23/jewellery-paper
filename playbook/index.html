For jewellery owners in Jaipur: Johari Bazar to Sitapura

The owner's playbook.

Old trade. New sums. Let's do them together.

Eight questions owners here ask most, each worked through with every calculation shown. The businesses in the examples are invented; the prices, rules and dates are real, and sourced.

  • 01Gold went up again. Do I raise making charges, or absorb it?Know what your work costs in rupees per gram; then you can see what a gold rise does to the bill, whichever way you charge.Read
  • 02Customers want the same look for less. Lighter, or 18-carat?₹2 lakh buys 13.4 g of 22K or 16.3 g of 18K. Show customers both and let them choose.Read
  • 03Fewer wedding dates this season. How much stock?11 dates instead of 17 can mean half as much again on each one. Stock for the busy days, and keep some money for January.Read
  • 04Hallmarking went up to ₹75. What does it cost me?Well under 1% of a typical gold piece. For silver it's closer to 1%, and it's your choice.Read
  • 05My stock's worth more, but cash is tighter. Why?Holding stock costs money every month and rides the gold price both ways. Count months, and know which bet you're making.Read
  • 06The US got harder. Where else can I sell?The UAE, UK, Australia, EFTA and Oman now take Indian jewellery at zero duty. Pick one and learn its paperwork.Read
  • 07Buyers want lab reports. When is it worth it?When the fee is a small share of the stone's value. Ask the buyer which lab first.Read
  • 08Foreign buyers come in winter. How do I plan the year?November brings about six times June's foreign visits. Order winter stock in September, and use summer for the work winter crowds out.Read
